Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what Afrishine Skills (Afrishine Academy and Afrishine Store) collects and why.

What we collect

  • Pi sign-in: your Pi username and a Pi user ID, confirmed through Pi Network. We never receive your Pi password or wallet keys.
  • Profile: the name you use and, if you add one, a profile photo.
  • Store activity: products you list (title, price, description, photos), bids, chat messages with buyers or sellers, and order records (product, amount, commission, buyer and seller IDs).
  • On your device: small settings and cached files so the app loads faster.

How we use it

To sign you in, run the Store (listings, chat, bids, payments, payouts), keep the service safe, and improve it. We do not sell your personal data.

Who processes it

  • Pi Network — sign-in and payments.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) — accounts and app data.
  • Cloudflare — storing and delivering photos and media.
  • Vercel — hosting the website.

What other members can see

Your username, Verified badge status, listings and bids are visible to other members. Chat messages are visible only to the two people in the chat and to Afrishine administrators when needed to resolve a problem or abuse.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep data while your account is active and as needed for order records. You can delete your own products in the app. To ask for other data to be deleted, email afrishinehub@gmail.com.

Children

Afrishine Skills is not directed to children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Questions or support: email afrishinehub@gmail.com.
